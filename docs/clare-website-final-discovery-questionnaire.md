1. Overall website reaction — 5 minutes

Cover this first because it tells you whether the whole direction is right.

Ask:

Does the website feel like you?

Does it feel professional enough for your business?

Does it feel suitable for both weddings and funerals?

What is your first honest reaction?

What feels right?

What feels wrong or not like you?

Decision needed:

Keep the current direction, or make a bigger design/style change.

2. Brand and visual style — 5 minutes

This affects every page, so confirm it early.

Ask:

Are you happy with the current logo/peacock feather direction?

Do the colours feel right?

Should the site feel more elegant, natural, modern, warm, romantic, calming, or premium?

Are there any colours, fonts, images, or styles you definitely dislike?

Decision needed:

Final design direction.

Move to messages later:

Exact font choices

Small colour tweaks

Alternative logo versions

Business card/social media matching

3. Homepage and page structure — 5 minutes

This confirms how visitors move through the site.

Ask:

Should the homepage lead with weddings, funerals, or you as Clare?

Should weddings and funerals have equal importance?

Does the current page list feel right?

Suggested launch pages:

Home

About

Weddings

Funerals

Reviews

Contact

Privacy

Ask:

Are any of these missing?

Should anything wait until after launch?

Decision needed:

Final launch page list.

Move to messages later:

Blog ideas

FAQ details

Naming ceremonies

Vow renewals

Extra ceremony pages

4. About Clare — 4 minutes

This makes the site personal.

Ask:

What do you want people to know about you before they enquire?

What made you want to become a celebrant?

What makes you different from another celebrant?

Are you comfortable mentioning your Police background?

If yes, should it be framed around calmness, professionalism, people skills, life experience, or service?

Decision needed:

How personal the About page should be.

Move to messages later:

Full About page wording

Personal phrases

Exact story details

Anything she wants removed from the biography

5. Weddings and funerals content — 6 minutes

This is the most important service content.

Ask for weddings:

What should couples feel when reading the Weddings page?

What is included in your wedding service?

Do you help with vows, readings, music, rituals, and ceremony structure?

Do you want the site to explain that celebrant-led weddings are separate from legal marriage registration?

Ask for funerals:

What should families feel when reading the Funeral page?

What is included in your funeral service?

Do you work directly with families, funeral directors, or both?

Do you help with eulogies, readings, music, poems, and tribute structure?

Are there any funeral words or phrases that feel too cold or insensitive?

Decision needed:

Core content for Weddings and Funerals pages.

Move to messages later:

Full process step-by-step

Exact ceremony timings

Detailed package descriptions

Symbolic ritual details

Full funeral director process

6. Pricing and enquiry form — 3 minutes

These are practical launch blockers.

Ask:

Do you want prices shown publicly, shown as “from” prices, or hidden until enquiry?

Do weddings and funerals need different pricing approaches?

What basic information do you need from someone enquiring?

Should the contact form be general, or should visitors choose Wedding, Funeral, or Other?

Where should enquiry emails go?

Decision needed:

Pricing visibility and contact form structure.

Move to messages later:

Exact package names

Exact prices

Travel fees

Deposits

Payment terms

Refund wording

Detailed form fields

7. Launch priorities — 2 minutes

End with this so you know what to implement first.

What must be finished before you feel comfortable launching?

What can wait until after launch?

What are the top three changes you want me to make first?
