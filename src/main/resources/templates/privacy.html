Privacy policy

Personal data

The site only asks for details needed to respond properly.

Information from enquiries and review submissions is used to reply, discuss ceremony requirements, moderate feedback, prepare services and keep appropriate business records. It is not sold.

01

Information collected

When you contact Clare's Life Celebrations, the website may collect your name, email address, phone number, ceremony type, preferred date, venue or location, and any details you include in your message.

If you submit a review, the website may also collect your display name, relationship to the ceremony, star rating, review heading and text, ceremony date, consent choice and any optional photographs you upload.

02

How information is used

Your information is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss ceremony requirements, provide relevant information about services, manage any next steps connected with your enquiry or booking, and moderate review submissions before publication.

03

Reviews and photos

Submitting the review form gives Clare's Life Celebrations permission to consider the submission for publication. If approved, the website may publish the name you provide, your relationship to the ceremony, rating, review, ceremony date and any photographs you upload.

Reviews are checked before publication and are never published automatically. You can ask for a published review or photograph to be corrected or removed by using the contact details below. Supporting records may be kept where reasonably needed to show that the review is genuine and that publication permission was provided.

04

How information is stored

Enquiry and review details may be stored in Clare's email system, website administration tools and related business records where needed to respond properly, prepare services, moderate content or keep appropriate administrative records.

05

Sharing information

Your data is not sold. Information is only shared where necessary to run the website, deliver communications, meet legal obligations or support a service you have asked Clare to provide.

06

Cookies and website data

This website may use essential website technologies needed for normal site operation. It also keeps simple aggregate visit counts, such as daily and weekly page visits, to help understand site activity without storing visitor names, IP addresses or personal profiles.

If broader analytics, cookie tools or embedded third-party services are added or changed later, this section should be updated to reflect them accurately.

07

Your rights

You can ask for access to the personal information held about you, request corrections, ask for deletion where appropriate, or raise concerns about how your information is used.

08

Contact details

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Clare's Life Celebrations by email at clarebruntoncelebrant@gmail.com.
