Destination weddings and the legal part

Celebrant-led ceremonies give you freedom, while the legal paperwork stays simple.

Clare can support destination wedding ceremonies across the UK and beyond. Whether you are planning East, West or anywhere in between, she can create a ceremony you will never forget, with travel logistics discussed clearly in your quote.

In England and Wales, the legal marriage service is completed separately with a registrar. Think of this as the paperwork: the rings, vows and meaningful words can be saved for your celebrant ceremony.
