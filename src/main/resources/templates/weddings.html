Legal and destination ceremonies

The paperwork stays simple. The ceremony gets to be meaningful.

In England and Wales, the legal marriage service is completed separately with a registrar. Your celebrant ceremony is where the rings, vows, promises and personal words can take centre stage.

Clare can support destination wedding ceremonies across the UK and beyond, with travel logistics discussed clearly as part of your quote.
