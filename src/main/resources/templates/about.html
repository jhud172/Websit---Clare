A little about Clare

Inspired by people, adventure and the moments that matter.

Clare lives in the North of England, surrounded by dramatic coastlines, beautiful countryside and rich history. She is passionate about adventure, travel and living for the moments that matter.

With 30 years' experience working closely with people during some of life's biggest moments, Clare brings a calm, confident and compassionate presence to every ceremony.

Her professional experience has strengthened her ability to build sincere connections, listen deeply and create a reassuring, supportive experience for every couple and family she works with.
