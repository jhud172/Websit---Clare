About Clare

Meet Clare Riley Brunton

A modern independent celebrant known for ceremonies that feel personal, sincere and professionally delivered.

Clare's approach

Trusted by couples, families and venues for a tone that is warm, genuine and easy to connect with.

Clare creates ceremonies that feel polished without ever feeling corporate. Every script is shaped around real people, personal stories and the atmosphere each occasion deserves.

Wedding ceremonies are modern, heartfelt and flexible. Funeral ceremonies are thoughtful, sensitive and dignified. In both settings, Clare's focus is the same: clear preparation, reassuring communication and meaningful delivery on the day.
